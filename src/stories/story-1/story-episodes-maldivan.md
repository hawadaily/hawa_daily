# Story Episodes: The Mafia's Stepmother

## Episode 1: The Beginning
**Content:** My husband kicked me out because I ate too much. Signing the divorce papers that night. I left with nothing with only a duffel bag and $36 in my pocket. I wandered the rainy streets alone. Then I spotted a flyer on a utility pole. Stepmother needed, room and board included. That exact flyer changed my life. It turned me into a woman far beyond my ex-husband's reach. That night, I followed the address and entered the estate of Na'eel Shareef, the head of New York's oldest mafia family.

Outside the mansion, the butler beside me oozed quiet arrogance. "The Dawn needs a mother for his child, not a wife. To get that straight, the Dawn is highly attractive. Don't get any ideas." I lowered my head, secretly delighted. Honestly, this job was perfect for me. In short, they just needed an upscale nanny, and the last thing I wanted was another husband. Free mansion lodging. At this point, that felt like a miracle. As we passed a room with its door ajar, a gunshot rang out and a man crumpled to the floor instantly. "The Dawn is handling some business. Don't take it personally."

I moved into the castle that same night. The butler led me to my room, then gave me one final warning. "Do not go to the third floor ever." His voice dropped. "The last woman who snuck up there and tried to get into Don Na'eel's bed, he threw her out of a second story window." I thought about the gun from an hour ago. I nodded hard. "I promise." I slipped into the silk pajamas laid out in the closet. "Damn it. I seriously need to lose weight."
